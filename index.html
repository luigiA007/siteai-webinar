What changed

People have learned to ask.

AI assistants taught a lot of people to type a whole question and get back one composed answer. Once you're used to that, a menu of pages feels like homework.

Real estate websites still hand visitors the menu. SiteAI gives them the answer, on the agent's own site, in the agent's own voice.

The philosophy behind it

TRIART

The Right Information At the Right Time.

The right information

Answer the question the visitor actually asked, using what the agent knows: their listings, their neighborhoods, their way of working.

At the right time

When the visitor asks, not three clicks later. And when they're ready to talk, the agent hears about it right away.

KirbyKeep it short. "Agent Image builds everything around one principle: TRIART, the right information at the right time. SiteAI is that principle applied to your whole website."

Built with guardrails

An AI that knows what it shouldn't say.

Real estate has rules, and SiteAI is built to follow them. It answers from your world, it stays inside Fair Housing, and you're always one step away.

Your own content

Answers come from your listings, your pages and licensed local-market information, not from the open web.

Fair Housing built in

Rules for protected classes and for how neighborhoods and schools are described are part of the product, not a prompt.

Clearly AI

You in the loop

Visitors can call or message you at any point. When they're ready, you take it from there.

Conversational commerce

The conversation is the new contact form.

A contact form gives you a name and "please contact me." A SiteAI conversation gives you the whole story: what the visitor wants, what they looked at, what they asked in their own words, and a suggested first line for the call.

That's what we mean by conversational commerce: the conversation ends in a qualified lead and a booked call with you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Isn't this just a chat window on my website?

No. A chat window replies in a little box in the corner. SiteAI builds a full page for each question, in your brand and from your own content, and then connects the visitor with you.

Where do the answers come from?

From your website: your listings, your bio, your service areas and licensed local-market information. It doesn't make up listings or pull from the open web.

What if it gets something wrong?

Visitors are told they're talking to AI that can make mistakes and to confirm details with you. It gives no legal or financial advice, and you can be reached at any point. If something needs changing, update the page it came from and SiteAI follows within a day.

How does it handle Fair Housing?

Fair Housing rules are built into the product. It describes places, never the people who live there, and points school questions to official sources. The brief tells you when it handled one of those questions.

What's in the intelligence brief?

Who the visitor is, what they want, what they looked at, their questions in their own words, what SiteAI handled for them, a readiness level (Exploring, Warm or Ready) and a suggested opening line.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. On a phone, the conversation opens as a panel the visitor can resize, styled like the rest of your site. The brief is the same either way.

Can it sound like me?

Today it matches your website's look, uses your name and speaks in a warm, professional tone. Tuning its voice to sound exactly like you is on the roadmap.

Will it hurt my search rankings?

No. Your existing pages stay in place and can still be found by search engines. SiteAI is a layer on top of your site.

Which websites can use SiteAI?

Agent Image websites, which for this launch means our WordPress sites. If your site was built by someone else, the path is an Agent Image website with SiteAI built in.

What do you need from me?

Usually nothing new. It answers from what's already on your website. We'll ask for the email address where your briefs should go.

Do I own it?

Your website, brand and content are yours. SiteAI is a subscription. If you ever cancel, it switches off and your website keeps working exactly as before.

Does it replace me?

No. Its job is to get the visitor to the right information and then to you, with a brief, so your first call starts in the right place.

What does patent pending cover?

Agent Image has filed a provisional patent application for the technology behind SiteAI. We won't go into the claims while it's pending.
